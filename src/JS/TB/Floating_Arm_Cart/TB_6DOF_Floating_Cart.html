The 6DOF Floating Arm Trebuchet with Moving Cart

This is the floating arm trebuchet with a sling. The 5 degrees of freedom are the x and y coordinates of the projectile, the height of the counterweight, and the sling and arm angles. To include the sling, a much more constraint-intensive (5DOF) approach is required.
